Services

I offer metocean engineering and project support services spanning marine environmental analysis, numerical modelling, operational forecasting, and the technical coordination of complex projects.

Metocean Services

Environmental characterisation and metocean data solutions for offshore, coastal, and marine infrastructure projects — wind, waves, currents, tides, and water levels.

Numerical Modelling

Regional and nearshore wave and ocean modelling with SWAN, WaveWatch III, and coupled ocean–atmosphere systems (COAWST, ROMS, WRF) — from setup and forcing to validation.

Hindcast & Forecast Support

Long-term hindcast production, operational wave forecasting, and statistical post-processing to turn model output into usable marine information.

Metocean Data Analysis

Quality control, time-series and extreme-value analysis, return-period estimation, and design-condition development from observations and reanalysis datasets.

Offshore Hydrodynamics

Wave–structure interaction and floating offshore wind analysis — added mass, radiation damping, RAOs, and extreme-condition response (WAMIT/HAMS, OpenFAST).

Project Support & Advising

Engineering project management, technical specifications, procurement and tendering support, contractor coordination, KPI and governance, and technical review for complex marine programmes.

How I can help

Whether you need a defined metocean deliverable, a validated wave model, an extreme-value assessment, or independent technical review and coordination on a marine or offshore project, I can help bridge the gap between scientific modelling, engineering interpretation, and project delivery.
