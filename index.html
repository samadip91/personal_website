About

I am a marine & ocean engineer and metocean professional with over ten years of experience spanning operational marine forecasting, numerical wave and ocean modelling, marine observation systems, and the engineering project management of national-scale marine infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of ocean science, engineering analysis, and project delivery — connecting field observations, environmental data, and numerical models into engineering interpretation, operational requirements, and implemented systems.

I have a B.Sc. in Oceanography from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) and an M.S. in Ocean Engineering from the University of Rhode Island, where my research focused on the hydrodynamic analysis of a 15 MW semisubmersible floating offshore wind turbine. I am currently involved in the Maritime Meteorological System Strengthening programme (MMS-2), an AFD-funded national marine observation and modelling programme.

Outside of work, I enjoy running, exploring coastal and marine environments, and learning about new modelling and computing tools.

Research & Professional Focus

My core professional domain bridges metocean engineering, marine observations, numerical modelling, and technical project coordination. Key themes include metocean and environmental data analysis, wave modelling (SWAN, WaveWatch III), coupled ocean–atmosphere modelling, extreme-value metocean analysis, marine observation-system implementation, and offshore hydrodynamics for floating offshore wind.

A distinctive part of my profile is acting as the interface between scientific users, engineers, procurement, contractors, consultants, and management — translating scientific and engineering requirements into measurable delivery and operational-performance criteria.

Selected Highlights

  • Engineering project coordination within the ~EUR 32M AFD-funded MMS-2 marine observation & modelling programme.
  • M.S. research on hydrodynamic coefficients and extreme-condition analysis of a 15 MW semisubmersible floating wind turbine (WAMIT/HAMS, OpenFAST).
  • Operational marine forecasting and metocean services experience with BMKG.
  • Instructor in WMO/OTGA-related metocean and marine observation training.
  • Field experience across hydrographic survey, buoy deployment, and marine instrumentation.
